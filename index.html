Where are you based?

We’re based in Mohali. We also serve Chandigarh, Panchkula, Punjab, Himachal and Kashmir.

Do you pick up from the airport?

Yes. We pick up from Chandigarh Airport and other airports on request, day or night.

Can I book a trip to Himachal or Kashmir?

Yes. We offer cabs and tours to Himachal, Kashmir, Spiti, Ladakh and more. Send us your dates and route on WhatsApp for a price.

How do I book a cab?

Send your pickup, drop-off, date and number of people on WhatsApp or call +91 87288 78910. We’ll confirm your car and price.

Can I book at any time?

Yes. You can book with us any time, day or night.

Can I book cars for weddings or work?

Yes. Tell us the date and how many cars you need on WhatsApp.

How much does a cab cost?

The price depends on your route, car and dates. Message us for a clear price before you book. No hidden fees.
