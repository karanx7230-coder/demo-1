Where is Karnail Tour and Travels based?

We are based in Mohali, Punjab, and serve Mohali, Chandigarh, Panchkula and the wider region — with outstation trips across Punjab, Himachal Pradesh and Kashmir.

Do you provide airport transfers?

Yes. Pickup and drop to Chandigarh airport (IXC) and other airports on request, including early-morning and late-night flights.

Can I book an outstation cab to Himachal or Kashmir?

Yes. Outstation cabs and multi-day tour packages are available for Himachal, Kashmir, Spiti, Ladakh and more. Share your route and dates on WhatsApp for a quote.

How do I book a cab?

WhatsApp or call +91 87288 78910 with your pickup, drop, date and passengers. We confirm the car, driver and fare before your trip.

Yes. We take bookings 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — early flights, late arrivals and last-minute plans included.

Do you provide cars for weddings and corporate travel?

Yes. Wedding transport, convoys, corporate travel and monthly billing. Tell us the date and the number of cars on WhatsApp.
